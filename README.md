## Featured Projects

### NexPass — Smart Access Control

**System Architecture · Embedded Systems · Hardware Design**

A smart access control platform I'm developing to connect physical access devices with centralized software.

My work covers much of the system, from the electronics inside the controller to the software that manages access and communicates with the devices.

- Designed the system architecture and communication protocols between controllers and the backend.
- Designed cryptographic protocols for NFC credentials.
- Worked on the electrical design and PCB development of ESP32-based access controllers.
- Developed firmware for credential validation, access decisions, and event handling.
- Built the integration between embedded devices and backend services.
- Implemented delta-based updates and incremental synchronization to exchange changes efficiently.
- Worked on offline operation and synchronization when connectivity becomes available again.

What makes this project interesting to me is that it brings several areas together. Hardware, firmware, security, and backend software all have to work as parts of the same system.

## How I Approach Engineering

I don't think good engineering starts with a diagram or a line of code. It starts with understanding the problem.

Before choosing a technology or designing a system, I believe it's essential to understand the people who will use it, how they work, what their environment looks like, and what actually needs to improve.

A technically impressive solution isn't necessarily a good solution. If it doesn't fit the users, their workflows, or the conditions in which it has to operate, then something important was missed.

Once that context is clear, architecture and technology become tools for solving the right problem.

I care about building systems that are understandable, secure, maintainable, and useful in the real world. Sometimes that means designing a complex solution; other times, it means finding a simpler way to get the job done.

**Understand the problem. Understand the people. Then build the right solution.**
